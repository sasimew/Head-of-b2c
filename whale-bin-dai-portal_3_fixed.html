❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I link my Game ID? +
Go to Member Center → Game IDs → Add New Game ID. Select your game, server, and enter your game ID. Click "Verify & Add" — a verification code will be sent to your in-game mailbox.
What if my top-up is pending? +
Pending payments usually resolve within 15 minutes. If it's still pending after 1 hour, please contact Payment Support with your Order ID.
Can I change my mobile number? +
Mobile number changes require identity verification. Please contact Customer Support with your Member ID and a valid ID document.
How do I reset my password? +
Click "Forgot Password" on the Login page. Enter your registered mobile number, verify the OTP, and set a new password.
Is my payment information secure? +
Yes. We do not store card details. All payments are processed through certified payment gateways (PromptPay, 2C2P, etc.) with industry-standard encryption.
